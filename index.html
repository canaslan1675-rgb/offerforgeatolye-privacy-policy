Privacy Policy – OfferForgeAtolye Pin Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This app is used only by the owner of the OfferForgeAtolye Etsy shop to publish Pins about the shop's own products to the owner's own Pinterest account.

Data we access

With the owner's authorization, the app reads the owner's boards and creates Pins on them. It does not access other Pinterest users' data.

Data storage and sharing

The access token is stored securely and used only to create Pins. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Contact

For questions, contact the shop owner through the OfferForgeAtolye Etsy shop page.
